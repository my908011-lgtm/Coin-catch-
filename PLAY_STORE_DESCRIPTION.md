# Coin Catch — Store listing draft

**Short description:** Tap coins, beat your score, and enjoy a quick arcade challenge!

**Full description:**
Coin Catch is a simple arcade game. Tap the coins as they appear and try to catch as many as you can before the 30-second timer ends.

Features:
- Quick 30-second rounds
- Tap controls
- Score and local high score
- Lightweight, simple gameplay

Coins are virtual points only. They have no cash value and cannot be redeemed for money or prizes.
